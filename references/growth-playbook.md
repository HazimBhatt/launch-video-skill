# Launch Video Growth & Viral Craft Playbook

Two proven launch arcs, selected by distribution channel:

## 1. The Viral X Craft Launch Arc (DEFAULT for `cinema-launch`)
Modeled after high-performing X / Product Hunt product films (Soar, Linear, Raycast) designed to earn organic bookmarks, quote-tweets, and link clicks in a muted feed:

| Phase | Window | Objective | Execution Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Stark Thesis Hook** | `0.0 – 2.0s` | Stop the scroll with sound OFF in `< 1.0s`. | 4–6 words max on stark architectural paper (`chromaticDecodeText`). Name the exact pain or bold capability immediately. Zero cold spinning logo, zero subtitle filler. |
| **2. Living World Entry** | `2.0 – 3.5s` | Signal Apple-grade craft and brand confidence. | Bright `mist` exposure bridge into a drifting sunlit photographic sky/studio plate (`livingPlateBg`). Reveal aspect-safe rounded emblem + crisp wordmark (`inlineEmblemSentence`). |
| **3. Tactile Macro Action** | `3.5 – 5.5s` | Show how effortless the core input is. | Dark Apple Liquid Glass `promptCapsule`, live keystrokes, optical **`2.6x–2.9x` macro push** right onto the submit button, Bezier macOS cursor click -> spinner morph + `120ms` audio pocket right before the beat drop. |
| **4. Proof Fan & Portal** | `5.5 – 12.5s` | Prove real product depth on the beat drop. | Radial `floatingCardFan` around frosted glass capsule -> collapse into macOS window -> horizontal pill-portal mask expand into real zoomed product screens + tactile physical `rubberStamp` verification hits. |
| **5. Signature Before/After 3D** | `12.5 – 19.2s` | Deliver undeniable visual transformation. | Dual 3D angled macOS windows (`E3D.renderDualCompare`) floating over transparent drifting sky with a real-time luminous optical scan-wipe transforming Before -> After live, followed by rapid centered capability cards. |

Beat-Drop Sprint & Mosaic Climax** | `22.6 – 28.5s` | Peak emotional momentum + full workspace proof. | 4 × 1-beat (`0.49s`) kinetic type cuts across contrasting photographic worlds -> **pixel-mosaic block dissolve** into the full product workspace annotated with 4 crisp `calloutBubble` pills & curved arrows. |
| **8. Editorial Meta Payoff** | `28.5 – 32.5s` | Memorable closing line that drives clicks & reposts. | Drums drop dead into warm reverb tail on stark paper: meta inline-emblem punchline -> transparent pricing/offer clarity -> bold domain lockup (`cohulo.com`). |

## 2. Direct-Response SaaS Arc (Optional for Paid Ads / `bento-proof` / `problem-story`)
1. **Pain Hook (0–3s)**: Quantify the wasted time/money in plain founder language.
2. **Instant Mechanism (3–12s)**: Real live UI showing the 1-step fix.
3. **Hard Proof & Comparison (12–22s)**: Real numbers, Before/After metrics, verified customer outcome.
4. **Risk-Reversed CTA (22–30s)**: Transparent price anchor + immediate action URL.

---

## Banned Copy & Visual Clichés (Instant Reject)
- **Zero em dashes (`—`)** anywhere on screen.
- **Zero banned AI words**: *revolutionize, seamlessly, empower, unlock, supercharge, elevate, cutting-edge, next-generation, streamline, game-changer, effortless, leverage, harness*.
- **Zero synthetic filler**: No corner code clouds (`scatterSyntaxCloud`), no generic shield grids (`matrixGrid`), no Hormozi yellow cartoon subtitles, no corner TV watermarks, no squashed logos, no blurred placeholder text.
